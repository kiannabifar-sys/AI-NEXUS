THE BIG PICTURE

So, what exactly is AI?

Artificial intelligence is a field of computer science focused on building systems that can perform tasks commonly associated with human intelligence, such as understanding language, recognizing patterns, generating content, and making predictions.

◈

Learn from Data

Many AI systems find patterns in examples instead of relying only on hand-written rules. The quality and variety of data matter enormously.

Machine LearningPatterns
◎

Recognize & Analyze

AI can classify images, transcribe speech, summarize documents, and help analyze complex information—while still making mistakes.

Create New Things

Generative AI can produce drafts, images, music, code, and ideas based on patterns learned during training and the instructions it receives.

INSIDE THE MACHINE

Meet the technologies powering tomorrow.

AI is not one single technology. Different methods can be combined to solve very different problems.

  • ✓
    Machine Learning
    Algorithms learn patterns from data to support predictions or decisions.
  • ✓
    Deep Learning
    Multi-layer neural networks help tackle complex tasks such as speech and image recognition.
  • ✓
    Large Language Models
    Models trained on large text collections can generate language, explain concepts, and assist with writing or coding.
  • ✓
    Computer Vision & Robotics
    Systems interpret visual information and help machines interact with the physical world.

A BRIEF HISTORY

From early ideas to everyday AI.

AI developed over decades through new ideas, computing power, larger datasets, and many waves of research.

1950s

The Big Question

Researchers began exploring whether machines could demonstrate aspects of intelligent behavior. The term “artificial intelligence” gained prominence in 1956.

1980s–2000s

Learning from Data

Expert systems, statistical methods, and machine learning research continued to evolve as computers and datasets improved.

2010s

Deep Learning

Neural networks made major progress in image recognition, speech, translation, and other complex tasks.

2020s

Generative AI

Generative models brought text, image, audio, and coding capabilities to a much wider audience.

THINK RESPONSIBLY

Powerful technology needs human judgment.

AI can be useful, but it is not magic—and it is not always right. Responsible use means understanding both its potential and its limits.

  • ✓
    Check important claims
    AI can produce confident-sounding errors. Verify facts with trustworthy sources.
  • ✓
    Protect private information
    Do not share sensitive information without understanding the service's data policies.
  • ✓
    Keep people involved
    Use AI as an assistant, not a replacement for human responsibility and judgment.
  • ✓
    Look for fairness
    Training data and system design can reflect biases. Consider who may be affected.
